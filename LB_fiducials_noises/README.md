## Noise descriptions ##

### LiteBIRD
* ```noise_litebird_b30.dat```: noise from PTEP residuals
* ```noise_litebird_nilc_b30_f80.dat```: noise from NILC residuals available in ``litebird_residuals`` with beam 30arcmin and 80% of the sky (E-mode group, march 2025)

### CMB-S4
* ```S4_CMB_noise.dat```: white noise for 1muK.arcmin
* ```s4wide_ilc_residuals.dat```: foreground residuals after ILC ([github](https://github.com/sriniraghunathan/DRAFT/tree/master/products/20220726))

### Simons Observatory

* ```SO_goal_noise_standard_ILC.txt```: noise+residuals after ILC (ref?)
* ```sogoal_ilc_residuals.dat```: noise+residuals after ILC ([github](https://github.com/sriniraghunathan/DRAFT/tree/master/products/20220726))

### Planck
Help for usage of the Planck-lite data can be found in ```planck_lite_usage_example.py```.

* ```noise_planck_lite.dat```: binned-covariance from the Planck lite likelihood
* ```noise_planck_bin_ref.dat```: bin definition for the Planck lite covariance

### Lensing
* ```S4_lensing_noise_nlkk.dat```: lensing noise curve for CMB-S4
* ```SO_lensing_noise_nlkk.dat```: lensing noise curve for Simons-Observatory
